# Placement360 🎓

Placement360 is a branch-aware placement readiness platform designed to help engineering students understand and improve their placement preparation.

Unlike a one-size-fits-all placement system, Placement360 considers the student's engineering branch and provides relevant skills, assessments, projects, and preparation roadmaps.

## 🎯 Branch-Aware Preparation

Placement preparation is different for different engineering branches.

Placement360 provides relevant preparation based on the student's branch instead of treating every student as a CSE student.

Examples include:

- Computer Science / IT – Programming, DSA and technical skills
- ECE – Electronics and communication-related skills
- EEE – Electrical and core engineering skills
- Mechanical – Mechanical and manufacturing-related skills
- Civil – Civil engineering and construction-related skills
- Other branches – Branch-specific preparation areas

## 🔄 How It Works

1. Student creates an account.
2. Student enters academic and profile information.
3. Student selects their engineering branch.
4. Placement360 provides branch-specific preparation areas.
5. Student completes the placement assessment.
6. Student records communication confidence separately.
7. Student adds projects and practical experience.
8. Dashboard generates a placement readiness indication.
9. Student follows the personalized preparation roadmap.

## 📊 Placement Readiness

The dashboard combines different aspects of placement preparation such as:

- Academic performance
- Assessment performance
- Communication confidence
- Projects
- Practical exposure
- Backlogs

The resulting value is intended as a **readiness indicator**, not a guaranteed placement probability.

The goal of Placement360 is to provide students with a simple and personalized way to understand where they currently stand in their placement preparation and what they should work on next.
